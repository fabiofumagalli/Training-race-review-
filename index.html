0.0 s
Speeds are an approximate IQFoil model in knots. The upwind angle changes with the wind: wider in light air to keep the board foiling, higher as the wind builds. Real race: the pin end must hold starboard before tacking, boards in the middle sail in bad air, and the whole fleet queues at mark 1. Class references: iQFOiL Race Management Guideline.
